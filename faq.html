Common Questions

Are your products original?

Yes, absolutely! We guarantee that all our products, including Apple, Samsung, and other brands, are 100% genuine and sourced directly from verified distributors. We don't sell copies or refurbished items unless explicitly stated.

How do I place an order?

Placing an order is extremely simple. Add the items you like to your cart and hit the "Order on WhatsApp" button. We will immediately receive your order details and confirm the delivery process with you directly.

How long does delivery take?

We pride ourselves on fast doorstep delivery. Generally, orders are dispatched within 24 hours. Depending on your location within India, delivery usually takes between 3 to 5 business days.

What payment methods do you accept?

We accept UPI, Net Banking, Credit/Debit Cards, and Cash on Delivery (COD) for select pin codes. Payment details are securely shared when you confirm your order via WhatsApp.

Can I track my order?

Yes! Once your order is dispatched, we will share a tracking link and courier details with you on WhatsApp so you can monitor your package until it arrives at your doorstep.
